I was at dinner with my girlfriend and she picked the restaurant based on a TikTok video. We were looking at the menu and couldn't decide what we wanted so we looked at the reviews on Google to see what dishes other people had posted about. After we picked out food, I thought about how much volunteerism there is on the internet. There is so much participation based on this need to share with no tangible reward. This participation can take the form of leaving a star rating or going further to write a review and add photos of the dishes one had, making a post on Reddit sharing their experiences/telling a story, or even just leaving comments on TikTok's and Instagram reels. Of course, some are paid to promote, or make content, but participation by us isn't necessary. In the grand scheme, there is no benefit to posting, one's life may not improve at all for leaving that message or comment, but the impact can be great! If I hadn't seen all the reviews saying get the chicken wings at that restaurant, I wouldn't have skipped out on them. There have been many times I found the comments as entertaining as the videos themselves or learned something cool from a random Reddit post someone made. I think that aspect of the internet is super cool.

I was talking to some of my lab mates about this and one of them mentioned that this reminds them of the [1% rule (The Wikipedia Article)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1%25_rule). This rule states that in an internet community, 1% create new content, 9% participate in changing or updating the content and 90% consume the content. It's crazy to think about how much influence the 10% of people creating content and leaving comments/reviews can have on the rest of the 90%.
