Memento is another amazing unreliable narrator movie. In the same vein as Shutter Island and as many twists as The Departed. I really like it. It was full of misdirection and confusion by the movie structure, dialogue and setting. There are so many details I probably missed that would be worth multiple rewatches.

I HIGHLY recommend this movie, which is why I'm giving it a 5/5.
