What can I say about this movie. The last children's movie I remember really enjoying was Finding Dory in 2016 and then The Incredibles 2 in 2018. Aside from that I've seen Sing and thats about it. The Wild Robot rekindled my love for children's movies. It elicited emotions me that I haven't felt in a long time. I don't think I've ever cried during a movie before and during this movie I cried 4 times. I got very attached to all the characters, the voice acting was great and the animation was beautiful. I'd call it a masterpiece, 5/5. Next week's movie is Apollo 13.
