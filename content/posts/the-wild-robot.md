What can I say about this movie, its been a long time since I've watched a proper childrens movie, grew up on the old Disney, Pixar and Dreamworks movies and I adore them. Children's movies feel like just spin-offs and slop now. Of course there have been diamonds released but I hadn't given them the chance yet. The last couple children's movies I've seen have been Finding Dory in 2016, Incredibles 2 in 2018 and Sing last year. The Wild Robot rekindled my love for children's movies. It elicited emotions me that I haven't felt in a long time. I don't think I've ever cried during a movie before and during this movie I cried 4 times. I got very attached to all the characters, the voice acting was great and the animation was beautiful. I am reinspired to try children's movies again, I've been told to watch the Puss in Boots movie so thats going on the backlog. Anyway I'd call The Wild Robot a masterpiece, 5/5.
