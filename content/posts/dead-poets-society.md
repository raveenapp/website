*Spoiler Free*

I loved this movie. As someone who was highly pressured to go into STEM and is slowly growing his appreciation for art and literature, I resonated with a lot of the messaging of the movie. The movie follows a group of students at an all boys prep school as the new year begins. They are taught by a new english teacher, Mr. Keating who is an alumni of the school. Mr. Keating opens their eyes to what it means to create and appreciate art. The movie depicts themes of conformity vs freedom, expressing ones self, following your passions and appreicating the arts. Though there's alot I like about the movie, in the lens for modern day some aspect's dont hold up as well. Womens only roles were to be characters of beauty and to be obtained which led to a scene that in the time was probably meant to be romantic but comes off more sexual assualt-y. Trigger warning, the movie also contain suicide if that is something you can't watch.

The movie is explicit in its messaging and I think it does it well. The sciences and law is important for survival but art and creativity is what it means to be human. The pursuit of the arts should not be scoffed cause thats where culture and an appreciation for life is developed. I give the movie a 5/5. Next weeks movie is Call Me By Your Name.

I enjoyed themes and developments of the characters:
- conformity vs freedom
  - The movie starts with an introduction to the setting of the prep school
  - the theme of conformity vs freedom, Neil is forced to focus on his education
  - neil in an act of rebellion revives the dead poet society and pursues acting 
  - he is forbidden to perform by his father
  - Mr Keating tells him he has to explain his passions to his father but instead neil lies and performs anyway, this leads his father to putting his son into boarding school and leads to neils suicide
 - "Sucking the marrow out of life doesn't 
		mean choking on the bone. Sure there's a 
		time for daring and there's a time for 
		caution, and a wise man understands 
		which is called for. "
  - both neil and his dad acted "daringly" and it lead to both choking on bones. Neil doesn't get to act and his father loses his son

Expression of ones self
- We are introduced to Todd Anderson his room mate who is a timid character, unable to speak up for himself
- he is challenged to read aloud and refuses to
- Keating brings it out of him and shows him he has to potential to create and speak
- Keating encourages Neil to express him self and to speak passions, 
- I think in the final scene of the movie when Todd pleads for forgiveness, KEating had a moment where he was deciding if he wanted to be daring or cautious and he chose to be cautious. Todd on the other had chose to be daring, which inspired the rest of his class to do the same, leading him to be the character who he said he couldn't be
