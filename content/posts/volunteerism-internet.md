I was at dinner with my girlfriend and she picked the restaurant that she picked based off a Tiktok video. We were looking at the menu and couldn't decide what we wanted so we looked at the reviews on Google to see what dishes other people had posted about. After we picked out food, I thought about how much volunteerism there is on the internet. There is so much participation based on this need to share with no tangible reward. This participation can take the form of  leaving a stars rating or going further to write a review and add photos of the dishes one had, making a post on reddit sharing their experiences/telling a story, even just leaving comments on Tiktok's and instagram reels. In the grandscheme, there is no benefit to posting, ones life may not improve at all for leaving that message or comment, but impact can be great! If I didn't see all the reviews saying get the chicken wings at that restaurant, I wouldn't have skipped out on them. There have been many times I found the comments as entertaining as the videos themselves, or learned something cool from a random reddit post someone made. I think that aspect of the internet is super cool
